Financial Services — now discontinued

Effective 1 Oct 2025 Sony spun off Sony Financial Group (SFGI), distributing 1 SFGI share per Sony share and retaining 16.40% at spin-off (a later ~17.4% voting figure is Unverified). It is a discontinued operation from Q1 FY2025 and equity-method from Q3 FY2025; by Q1 FY2026 it no longer met equity-affiliate criteria. Total assets fell ¥35,293.2B (Mar 2025, incl. finance) → ¥15,683.5B (Mar 2026) — a deconsolidation, not a sale. Including discontinued ops, FY2025 attributable result was a loss of ¥326.9B — never read that as “Sony lost money” operationally. A ¥43.9B land gain was crystallised on the spin-off.

4b   Downside Risks

  • PlayStation cycle cliff: PS5 is 6 years old; cumulative 95.3m. PS6 timing is unverified (rumours of late 2027/early 2028 are not guidance). 2027–28 is the classic air-pocket.
  • Memory-price shock: Management flags “continued surge in memory prices” for ET&S/G&NS — hits PS5 BOM, cameras and TVs at once.
  • Kumamoto earthquake, 28 Jul 2026 (M7.1): Kumamoto Tech Center halted; gradual restart from 4 Aug, pre-quake output by mid-Aug. Sony excluded the impact from its raised FY2026 forecast because it could not reasonably estimate it — an open liability until Q2 quantifies it. Sensor lead times of 10–20 weeks are Unverified.
  • FX — ADR double exposure: Weak yen flatters yen results but a snap-back to ~¥140/$ would cut reported USD earnings ~10%+ with no business change — modelled sensitivity, not a forecast.
  • US tariffs: Q1 G&NS was helped by tariff refunds (amount undisclosed); PS5 prices were raised in Aug 2025 / Apr 2026. Reversal hits hardware first.
  • Regulatory / geopolitical: Music royalty & catalogue scrutiny, games platform-fee/content rules, AI-training-data rules, China (~11%+ of sales) + Taiwan-Strait / Kyushu concentration. No Sony-specific fine is asserted — these are sector headwinds.
  • Execution: Bungie (¥138.4B of FY2025 one-offs incl. Bungie) and Pixomondo show M&A/content bets can destroy capital.

Customers — SONY (B2C note)

Consumer-facing: no top-7 business-customer list. The majority of sales are to consumers (PlayStation hardware/software, music, pictures), although image sensors are sold to business customers (notably phone makers) in aggregate; no single customer is disclosed above 10% of group revenue in the pack. Key demand drivers: PlayStation cycle and software attach, sensor demand from smartphone makers, and music/pictures content performance.

AI trade positioning — SONY

Role: Beneficiary. Sony is an AI beneficiary at one remove: image sensors feed AI vision systems, PlayStation and content benefit from AI tools, and the pack's pivot is entertainment/IP at 67% of sales — but Sony does not sell AI infrastructure itself.

While the AI trade runs. While the AI trade runs, demand grows through sensor orders from smartphone makers and through content/IP monetisation; the multiple is supported by the entertainment mix, not an AI-infrastructure premium.

When the AI trade unwinds. If the trade unwinds, demand breaks first in sensors if phone makers cut orders, and in content spending; then the multiple compresses toward a through-cycle conglomerate multiple (the pack's continuing-operations band is ~20–23x); then the balance sheet — near-zero net debt on StockAnalysis' ex-finance definition — is a cushion. Watch: sensor lead times and pricing, the Q2 FY2026 print's margin, and any Kumamoto/memory disruption.
