AI Trade Dependency Graph — Audited (Nov 2026)

How to read this: money and demand flow top → bottom. Arrows point from supplier to customer: A → B means “A supplies B”. Hover (or tap) any node to see what it contributes, who it depends on, and who depends on it. Click a node to pin its card, then “Isolate neighbourhood” to dim everything except that node and its direct suppliers and customers. Click any arrow to see that relationship’s audit record: what is supplied, whether it is direct or indirect, the confidence level, and the source. Every edge in this rebuild was re-checked in a Nov 2026 audit of the first draft (60 nodes, 128 edges): 3 edges were removed, 86 added, and the rest kept or relabelled. Edges marked unverified are relationships where no direct contract was found — they are shown as unverified, never as non-existent.

Note on the v2 intrinsics shown here: the weighted intrinsic averages DCF / dividend / book-value methods, and the book-value floor drags it down for asset-light names — so most names screen at a large “premium” to it. For those names the DCF value in the card is the primary read. See the v2 reports for the full methodology. Hover financials are unchanged from the first draft; a separate pending decision (single-source Peter Lynch fair value for the 124 ticker files) is not yet applied here.

Status vocabulary: direct = the supplier sells to the customer itself; indirect = the flow passes through an intermediary (e.g. an OEM, a JV, or another supplier’s product); confirmed = company disclosure or a named contract; reported = credible press/analyst reporting, not company-confirmed; inferred = structural or set-level evidence, no named deal; unverified = no direct evidence found, shown rather than dropped.

Relationships: every edge carries a source, a confidence level (high / medium / low, with high-medium, medium-high and low-medium gradations), and a status (direct vs indirect, and confirmed / reported / inferred / unverified evidence). “Reported” means credible press or analyst reporting, not a company confirmation. “Unverified” means no direct evidence was found in this pass — the edge is shown as unverified, never deleted as if the relationship did not exist. Revenue shares are not shown anywhere because most companies do not disclose them — the graph shows that a dependency exists, not its size. Edge thickness does not encode size.
