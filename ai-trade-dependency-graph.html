AI Trade Dependency Graph — First Draft

How to read this: money and demand flow top → bottom. Arrows point from supplier to customer: A → B means “A supplies B”. Hover (or tap) any node to see what it contributes, who it depends on, and who depends on it. Click a node to pin its card, then “Isolate neighbourhood” to dim everything except that node and its direct suppliers and customers. This is a draft for iteration — relationships marked “reported” are not yet confirmed, and prices are 30 Oct 2026 closes unless stated otherwise.

Note on the v2 intrinsics shown here: the weighted intrinsic averages DCF / dividend / book-value methods, and the book-value floor drags it down for asset-light names — so most names screen at a large “premium” to it. For those names the DCF value in the card is the primary read. See the v2 reports for the full methodology.

Pick a node

Hover or tap any node in the graph. Its suppliers (“depends on”) and customers (“supplies to”) will highlight, and the rest will dim.

Start with NVDA (the centre of the trade), TSM (the bottleneck below it), or OpenAI (the demand at the top) — then use “Isolate neighbourhood” to trace one company's whole chain.
